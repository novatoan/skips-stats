HOW TO USE THIS APP

This app keeps stats for a Goaltimate game while you watch it. You tap a player's tile every time they catch the disc, and the app works out touches, goals, assists, turnovers and playing time for you. Hold the iPad sideways.

1Set up the game

  • Tap NEW GAME.
  • Choose ONE TEAM to track just your team, or TWO TEAMS to track both.
  • Type each team's name, pick a color, and add players (name and number). Press Return to jump to the next row.
  • Optional: tap the camera next to a player to add their photo.
  • Optional: SAVE ROSTER keeps a team for next time; LOAD ROSTER brings it back.
  • Tap START GAME.

2Start the clock

  • Tap ▶ at the top right when play begins, and again to pause.
  • If you record a touch while the clock is stopped, it flashes yellow as a reminder.

3Tap whoever has the disc

  • Every time a player catches the disc, tap their tile. A green circle shows who has it.
  • That's it — passes, touches and who's on the field are all worked out from your taps.

4Record goals and turnovers

  • GOAL (or 2 PT GOAL): the player holding the disc scores, and the player who threw it to them gets the assist. The scorer's team keeps the disc.
  • TURN: pick what happened — Throwaway, Drop, Stall or D. The disc goes to the other team.
  • After a D, the app asks WHO GOT THE D? — tap that defender.
  • Made a mistake? UNDO takes back the last thing you did. Tap it again to go further back (up to 40 steps).

5Keep track of who's on the field

  • Bright tiles are on the field; dimmed tiles are off. Each team can have 4 on at once.
  • Tapping a player puts them on automatically. To sub someone on or off by hand, tap the small ● / ○ in the corner of their tile.
  • Getting this right is what makes playing time and +/- accurate.
  • EDIT TEAM lets you add, remove or rename players (and the team) mid-game.

6Finish and get your stats

  • Tap END, then VIEW STATS.
  • EXPORT CSV or EXCEL saves a spreadsheet with every player's stats, who threw to whom, and a timestamped log of the whole game. BACKUP saves a complete copy of everything.
  • Every game is saved automatically as you play, so nothing is lost if the app closes. Find old games under PAST GAMES on the home screen and export them any time.

What the stats mean

TCH
Touches — times the player had the disc
AST
Assists — threw the pass that scored
TO
Turnovers — charged to the last player who had the disc
D
Defensive block that won the disc back
POSS WON
Times the other team lost the disc while this player was on the field
POSS LOST
Times this player's team lost the disc while they were on the field
+/-
Points scored minus points allowed while on the field
TIME
Playing time (counts only while the clock runs)

PAST GAMES

Games are saved automatically as you play and stay on this device until you delete them. CSV is one sheet. EXCEL has a tab for each section. BACKUP is a complete copy of everything recorded.
